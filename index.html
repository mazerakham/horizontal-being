Horizontal Being

You live in the first Heisenberg group. You can only move horizontally, and you see along horizontal geodesics.
(x,y,t)·(x′,y′,t′) = (x+x′, y+y′, t+t′+2(xy′−yx′))
W S drive, A D steer (turn in place when stopped), Q E strafe, Shift slow
Click the eye to ask what a road reaches and lay down one full turn of it, F follows that road, C clears roads, L starts a new loop count
M map, G god view, H pure eye, [ ] steer curvature, R restart, Esc menu
The eye: across is the direction a road sets out in, with straight ahead in the middle. Up and down is how hard the road curves, left turns above the bright line and right turns below it. Each pixel shows the first ball its road reaches. Counterclockwise loops lift you by 4 times the area they enclose.
Press Enter to play, or 1 to 4 to pick a level.
